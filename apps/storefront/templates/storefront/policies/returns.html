Damaged, wrong or missing

If anything arrives damaged, isn't what you ordered, or never arrives, write to us within 30 days of delivery (or of the expected delivery) with your order number and, for damage, a photo. We send a replacement or refund it in full, including shipping; you don't need to send anything back.

Changed your mind

  • Tea: because it is food, we can't take back tea once it has left us. Unopened or opened, if a tea isn't to your taste, tell us: we'd like to know, and we will try to make it right.
  • Merchandise and accessories: return them unused and in their packaging within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item's price. Write to us first; return postage is yours unless the item was faulty.

Cancelling an order

Write to us as soon as you can. If we have not packed it, we cancel it and refund it in full. If we cannot ship by the date we promised, you may cancel for a full refund.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 days of our sending them, depending on your bank. We email you when we send one.
