Where we ship

The 48 contiguous states and the District of Columbia, by USPS. We don't yet ship to Alaska, Hawaii, US territories, military (APO/FPO/DPO) addresses or abroad.

When

We weigh and pack each order by hand when it comes in, and ship within {{ checkout.ship_within_days }} working day{{ checkout.ship_within_days|pluralize }} of payment. The checkout shows the date we promise; if we are away, it says so and gives the date we ship. If we can't make the date we gave, we email you a new one and you may cancel for a full refund. Then the post office's own times apply: the checkout shows each service's usual time in transit.

What it costs

The checkout shows the live USPS price for your parcel's weight and address{% if checkout.free_shipping_over is not None %}, and orders of {{ checkout.free_shipping_over|money }} or more (after discounts) ship free by USPS Ground Advantage{% endif %}. We don't add a handling charge.

Addresses

We check addresses with the post office's records when you order, and may suggest a corrected spelling. If you need to change the address, write to us before we ship.

Tracking, lost and damaged parcels

We email you the tracking number when the parcel leaves. If it is lost or arrives damaged, write to us: see returns and refunds.
