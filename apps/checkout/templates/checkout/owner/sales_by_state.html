New York sales tax collected, by filing quarter

Tea is exempt; merch and accessories, with their share of shipping, are taxed. These are New York's sales tax quarters (March to May, June to August, September to November, December to February), each due on the 20th of the month after. For the return itself, with taxable sales by jurisdiction and the annual filer's year, see the New York sales tax return report. The figures are net of refunds: a refund comes off the quarter it was issued in, not the one the order was paid in, as NY returns require, and takes back the taxable amount and tax of what it was for (none for tea; a refund not for particular items takes its share of the order's).

Sales into each state against its threshold

Once sales into a state pass its economic-nexus threshold you must register there and collect its sales tax. The thresholds are a starting summary (Checkout → Nexus thresholds in the admin): check a state's own rule when it is flagged. Sales are without sales tax and net of refunds, each refund taken off the year it was issued in; orders count those paid in the year and not cancelled.
