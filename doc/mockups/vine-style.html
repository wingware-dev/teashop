Assam Second Flush

Malty, brisk and generous with milk

A full, malty black tea with a honeyed finish; takes milk well.

Caffeine: high

Sizes and prices

Second-flush Assam is picked in early summer, when the leaves are at their richest. This one brews a bold cup that stands up to milk and sugar, and is just as good plain.

Origin

Grown in
India
Region
Assam
Garden
Summer (second flush) picking, upper Assam

Tasting notes

Deep copper cup with a malty body, a hint of dried fruit and a long, honeyed finish.

  • Honey
  • Brisk
